License
-------
MiSans is free for both personal and commercial use. Per the official
FAQ at https://hyperos.mi.com/font/zh/faq :

  * "MiSans Global 所有的字体都是供全球免费商用，您可以在任何平台、
    任何商业项目中使用所有字体。"
    (All MiSans fonts are free for commercial use worldwide, on any
    platform and in any commercial project.)
  * Embedding question ("是否可以用作嵌入式字体？"): "可以。但您应在
    软件中特别注明使用了 MiSans 字体。" — embedding is allowed, provided
    the software carries a prominent notice of MiSans usage. The notice
    required by that term lives at the top of ../style.css (imported by
    the app UI) and in this file.
  * Modification question ("我可以在软件中修改编辑、二次创作字体吗？"):
    "您可以自由调节字体的粗细、间距等。但您不得单独将 MiSans 字体或其
    组件进行外观上的更改。" — the restriction targets standalone
    re-issuing of altered fonts. The files here are ordinary webfont
    subsets: glyph outlines are byte-for-byte unchanged, no component's
    appearance was altered, and they are never distributed standalone —
    only as part of the EasyTier application, which the license
    explicitly permits.

Subsetting record
-----------------
Produced 2025-08 with fontTools (pyftsubset 4.63.0, woff2/brotli):

  * Latin slices  : U+0000-00FF, U+0100-017F, U+2000-209F, U+20A0-20BF,
                    U+2122, U+2190-21BB, U+21D2, U+2212, U+2248,
                    U+2264-2265, U+25A0-25FF
  * CJK slices    : 3,756 Han characters (GB2312 Level-1 table, 3,755
                    glyphs, plus every additional Han character found in
                    EasyTier GUI / web-console / locale sources), plus
                    CJK punctuation U+3000-303F, fullwidth forms
                    U+FF01-FF60 / U+FFE0-FFE6 and U+2103 (℃).
  * Hinting instructions removed; kern kept; woff2 (brotli) compressed.

To regenerate, rerun pyftsubset against the official MiSans TTFs with the
same options.
